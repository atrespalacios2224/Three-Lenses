Three Lenses

Objective: Analyze one historical photograph and use evidence to connect it to environment, economy, or government.

EnvironmentHow do environment and geography affect our lives?
EconomyTo what extent does the American economy shape the American experience?
GovernmentHow does democracy shape America?
Step 1

Choose one image to investigate

Look across all five photographs. Select the one that raises the most questions for you. You will study it closely for the rest of the lesson.

3 minutes
Select an image to begin.
Your choice can be changed until you begin Step 2.
Step 2

Look closely before reading the context

Slow down. Record only what the photograph itself shows, then make one evidence-based inference.

8 minutes
I notice ___, ___, and ___.
The most important detail is ___ because ___.
I would ask/hear ___ because the image shows ___.
Complete all three observations.
Step 3

Connect the image to a unit theme

Read the historical context. Decide which lens best explains the image. More than one lens may fit, but choose the strongest.

9 minutes
Historical context

Which lens is strongest?

The strongest lens is ___ because the image shows ___. The context explains ___.
Environment: land, climate, resources, disasters, migration
Economy: work, wages, production, opportunity, hardship
Government: rights, laws, public policy, democracy, protest
Choose a lens and explain it.
Step 4

Compare without a partner

Choose one different image. Compare it to your main image, then decide whether your original theme choice is still the strongest.

7 minutes

Choose a comparison image

Then explain one similarity, one difference, and whether your original theme choice changed.

Both images show ___. Unlike my image, the comparison image shows ___. My theme choice did/did not change because ___.
Choose and compare one different image.
Step 5

Answer the essential question

Use your image as evidence. Write one complete paragraph with a claim, specific evidence, and reasoning.

13 minutes

○ Image evidence
○ Explanation
